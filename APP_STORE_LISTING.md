# App Store listing

The App Store listing for `app.mediagg` on iPhone, kept beside `PLAY_LISTING.md` and made from it:
what the iPhone does not have taken out, what only the iPhone has put in. The same rule applies:
claim only what is in the app's source, in the App Store build (downloaders on, Mediagg Arr Stack
pairing and Hearr off; mediagg-kmp `gradle.properties`).

Not on the iPhone, so not here: SMB shares, Chromecast and DLNA, accent colours from the wallpaper,
reading the device's whole music library (the iPhone plays a folder you pick, and what is opened with
Mediagg), sharing to Mediagg from another app's share sheet, Android TV, and supporting development
(tips, badge, icons: hidden on iOS since 2026-10-30). Add Apple TV when it ships on this record.

## Description

```
Mediagg plays music, podcasts, audiobooks, radio and video from your own media server, the files on your iPhone and anything on the web with an address. One library, one player, one queue.

YOUR OWN MEDIA SERVER
Mediagg is a client for Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, Audiobookshelf, Kodi and Subsonic servers (Navidrome, Airsonic, Gonic and others), and for WebDAV shares on a NAS, Nextcloud or computer. Your library stays where it is, and your progress is reported back so your other players stay in step.
• Continue watching, Next up, recently added and most played shelves, or the full library
• Sign in to several servers and browse them as one combined library
• Mixes built from your server's own suggestions, one tap to start
• Skip intros, recaps and credits on server video

PLAYS WHAT OTHERS WON'T
A second playback engine opens what Apple's player can't: MKV, several audio tracks, DTS and E-AC-3 sound, PGS and ASS subtitles.

FILMS AND SERIES REQUESTS
Connect Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr, Readarr, Prowlarr, Bazarr and Seerr, with qBittorrent, Transmission, Deluge or SABnzbd.
• Discover films and series and request them through Seerr
• Queue, calendar, missing titles and disk space at a glance
• Jump from a film in Radarr straight to watching it on your server

AUDIOBOOKS
• Chapters, bookmarks, sleep timer to the end of a chapter, and playback speed
• Audiobookshelf libraries, or M4B and MP3 books on your iPhone

PODCASTS
• Search several podcast directories at once, or browse top podcasts by country and category
• Add any feed by address, open a podcast link from a website or message, or import an OPML file
• Chapters, show notes and transcripts when an episode has them
• Automatic downloads with a limit, clearing older episodes to make room
• New episodes fetched in the background
• Keep subscriptions and positions in sync with gpodder.net or Nextcloud

RADIO AND LIVE STREAMS
• Play internet radio and live streams from M3U and PLS playlists or any stream address
• Keep a station list up to date as a subscription, or save what is in it now as a playlist
• Works with a self-hosted m3ugoat server for your own arranged playlists

YOUR FILES
• Pick a folder of music and video, or open files with Mediagg from the Files app and other apps
• Timed lyrics from your files or from LrcLib, following the music line by line
• Playlists from M3U files or addresses

ONE PLAYER FOR EVERYTHING
• A podcast episode, a track from your files, a live station and a film on your server can all sit in the same queue
• Mini player that opens into the full player, with the queue one more step away
• Lock screen and Control Center controls, and playback that carries on in the background
• Video in the player or in picture in picture
• AirPlay to your Apple TV, speakers and AirPlay televisions
• Search everything at once: your files, your servers and your subscriptions

OPENS STRAIGHT FROM THE WEB
Podcast, station list and OPML links through mediagg.app, and podcast links, open in Mediagg in one tap. What you share from Mediagg opens for anyone, with the app or without.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light, dark, or true black
• Accent colours from what is playing, or one of eight colours
• Choose which shelves Home shows, and in what order
• Listening statistics: time played per show, space used per download, and month by month
• Back up your library, play state and queue to a file, and restore it on another device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Mediagg talks to your servers and to the feeds and services you choose, and nothing else. Crash logs stay on your iPhone unless you decide to send them.

Mediagg is not affiliated with Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, Audiobookshelf, Kodi, Navidrome, Sonarr, Radarr, Seerr or any other server or service it connects to. You need your own server, or access to one, to use the media server features.
```
