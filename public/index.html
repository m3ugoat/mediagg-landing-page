Everything in one place

Built for people with real libraries

Not a streaming subscription. A player that respects the music you already own, the shows you already follow, and the server you already run.

🎵

Local media, properly organised

Everything on your device, sorted the way a music app should sort it — not dumped into one list.

  • Songs — every track, sortable by title, date or duration
  • Albums — real album art, filters and multi-select
  • Videos — local video with a full-screen player
  • Files — a genuine file browser with breadcrumbs and storage volumes
📚

Playlists that are yours

Build them in the app or bring the ones you already have. Nothing is locked in.

  • Create, rename, reorder and delete
  • Import M3U playlists straight from your storage
  • Add anything to a playlist from any screen
  • Play all or shuffle from the top of any list
🎙️

A serious podcast player

The full podcast experience, not an afterthought bolted onto a music app.

  • Subscriptions, queue, inbox and downloads
  • Chapters and transcripts with follow-along
  • Automatic downloads and smart queue rules
  • OPML import/export and gpodder.net sync
🎬

Video, in the same player

The films and shows on your phone and on your server, without a second app to open.

  • Local video with a full-screen player
  • Films and shows from Jellyfin, Plex and Emby
  • Continue watching picks up where you stopped
  • Cast any of it to the television
🎚️

Playback that behaves

The controls you actually reach for, where you expect them.

  • Variable speed, skip silence, sleep timer
  • Lyrics for local files and server tracks
  • Full queue control with swipe actions
  • Lock-screen and notification controls
🎨

Make it yours

Very few apps let you move this much furniture. This one does.

  • Reorder or hide every home-screen section
  • Bottom bar, side drawer, or both
  • Grid or list, and how many columns — per screen
  • Light, dark and true-black themes

Chromecast · DLNA · UPnP renderers
Send it anywhere

Casting that isn't fussy about brands

Chromecast is supported, and so is every DLNA/UPnP renderer on your network — the smart TV, the AV receiver, the old networked speaker that nothing else will talk to. Mediagg even runs its own media relay, so files on your phone and tracks on your server both reach the device.
