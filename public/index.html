Features

Built for your own media collection

Mediagg plays the media you own, the podcasts you follow and the content on the servers you run. No subscription is required.

🎵

Local media

Music, video and files on your device, organised into separate views.

  • Songs — sort by name, date added or length
  • Albums, artists, genres and years, built from your file tags
  • Videos — full-screen video player
  • Filesystem — file browser with support for multiple storage volumes
📚

Playlists

Create playlists in the app or import existing ones.

  • Create, rename, pin and add cover images
  • Import M3U playlists from device storage
  • Add items to a playlist from any screen
  • Play or shuffle any list
🎙️

Podcasts

A complete podcast player with subscriptions, downloads and sync.

  • Subscriptions, queue, inbox and downloads
  • Chapters and transcripts that follow playback
  • Automatic downloads that free up space as needed
  • OPML import and sync with gpodder.net or Nextcloud
🎬

Video

Play films and shows from your device and your media servers.

  • Local video playback
  • Films and shows from Jellyfin, Plex and Emby
  • Continue watching resumes where you left off
  • Cast to your TV
🎚️

Playback controls

Control how your media plays.

  • Variable playback speed and sleep timer
  • Synchronised lyrics from your files or LrcLib
  • Queue with drag-to-reorder, search and sorting
  • Lock-screen and notification controls
🎨

Customisation

Adjust the layout to suit how you use the app.

  • Reorder the home-screen sections
  • Choose the tabs in the bottom bar
  • Grid or list view, saved per screen
  • Light, dark and true-black themes, with colours taken from the artwork

● Android TV · Apple TV

On your TV, too

Mediagg has a TV app for Android TV and for Apple TV, built from the same code as the phone app and made for the remote. Android TV is on Google Play now, in the same listing as the phone app. Apple TV is on the way.

Add from phone

Set up the TV from your phone

No typing addresses and passwords with a remote. On the TV, open Add and choose Add from phone, and it shows a QR code. Point your phone’s camera at it: Mediagg opens on the phone with everything it has set up. Tick what the TV should take and press Send to the TV. The TV adds each one and says how each went.

Where a server allows it, the TV gets a sign-in of its own, approved from the phone, rather than your password. Works on Android TV and Apple TV, from an Android phone or an iPhone.

Mediagg Server · macOS and Linux

No server yet? Make one in three questions

Mediagg Server turns a Mac, a Raspberry Pi or any Linux computer into a home media server. One command installs what you choose — Jellyfin, Emby or Plex, Navidrome for music, Audiobookshelf for audiobooks, and the services that find and fetch more — sets each one up and connects them to each other. Everyone in the house gets a login of their own. Pairing — one six-digit code that hands the lot to Mediagg on each phone and TV — is coming soon to the Mediagg apps; until then, add each server in the app with its address.

$curl -fsSL https://mediagg.app/install.sh | sh

Paste it into a terminal — on the computer itself, or over SSH. Free and open source, runs in Docker, removable in one go. Windows to follow.

How it works

  1. 1
    Run one commandChoose a folder for your films. Docker is added if it is missing.
  2. 2
    Answer three questionsWhere you watch and listen, what fetches more — and a login for everyone in the house.
  3. 3
    Press InstallEach service is set up and connected for you, with a real progress bar.
  4. 4
    Pair Mediagg — coming soonOne code, typed or scanned, will add every server at once, signed in as that person. Until it reaches the apps, add each server in Mediagg with its address.

Casting

Chromecast and DLNA casting

Cast to Chromecast devices and to DLNA/UPnP renderers on your network, including smart TVs, AV receivers and networked speakers. While casting, Mediagg runs a built-in media relay, so files on your device can be played as well as streams from your servers.

Search and discovery

Search across all your sources

A single search covers your device, your podcast subscriptions and every connected server. Switch to Discover to search podcast directories instead.

🔍

Unified search

Search local files, podcasts and all connected servers at once, with results grouped by source.

🌍

Top podcasts

Browse the Apple Podcasts charts by country and category, starting with your region. Directory search also includes PodcastIndex.

⭐

Synced favourites

Starring a track in Mediagg also stars it on your server.

📊

Listening statistics

View your listening history by show, with monthly charts.

📺

Track, artist, album, film and show mixes generated by your server, plus a Surprise me section on Home with a random selection from your library.
