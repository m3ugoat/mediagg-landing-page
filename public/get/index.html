Get Mediagg

Where Mediagg runs.

The app on Android phones, Android TV and the Mac, the same app in your browser, and Mediagg Server if you want a server of your own.

Android phone and tablet

Available

Every kind of server, podcasts, downloads for when there is no connection, casting to Chromecast and DLNA.

Android TV

Available

The same Google Play listing as the phone. On the TV, find Mediagg in Google Play, or pick the TV from Google Play on the web. Set it up with Add from phone.

Mac

Available

Every film as it is, through VLC: MKV, HEVC, 4K, DTS and 5.1 surround, with nothing for your server to convert. Every kind of server but SMB, on your home network or plain http, found on the network by itself. Folders of your own music kept, and radio with what is playing. macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel; signed and notarised by Apple.

Download for Mac

In your browser

Available

Nothing to install. Sign in to Jellyfin or Navidrome on an https:// address; your sign-ins stay in that browser. On your own Mediagg Server it opens signed in to everything the server set up.

Open mediagg.app/web

Mediagg Server

macOS · Linux

A home media server, set up for you. Paste this into a terminal on a Mac, a Raspberry Pi or a Linux computer:

curl -fsSL https://mediagg.app/install.sh | sh

What works where

A browser only lets a web page reach servers that allow it, and never an http:// server from an https:// page. The apps have neither limit. Your own Mediagg Server reaches its own services for the page.
