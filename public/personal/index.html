What's different

A player that minds its own business

Same music, same podcasts, same servers, same casting. What changes is how much of your evening gets reported back to somebody else.

No ads

Not fewer ads. None.

No banner in your album list. Nothing between you and the play button, nothing that loads before your library does, and nothing you have to look at twice to find the X.

There is no paid tier that removes them either, because there is nothing to remove.

No bannersNo pop-ups No sponsored rowsNo upsell
🔇
Quiet by construction

The advertising code was never added to this edition, so there is no version of it that shows you one.

No tracking

What you play stays between you and the speaker

Most free apps quietly carry a hidden number that identifies your phone, so that what you do in one app can be matched with what you do in another. Personal has no such number to hand out.

It does not count your plays, does not time your sessions, and does not phone home when something goes wrong. Your listening history, your playlists and your statistics are worked out on the phone and stay on it.

Nothing sent about your libraryNo profile built No history uploadedNo error reports
Why it is different from a privacy setting

There is no switch, because there is nothing to switch off

A privacy toggle is only as good as your memory of finding it — and it can be moved, reset by an update, or quietly default back to on.

Personal takes the other route. The advertising and tracking parts are not included in the app at all, so they are not sitting there waiting for a setting to change. You do not have to trust a checkbox; there is simply nothing installed that could report on you.

Nothing to configureNothing to reset Nothing to trust
🚪
Left out, not turned off

The difference matters: a setting can change its mind after an update. Something that was never built in cannot.

Why radio and TV are the price. Browsing thousands of stations means asking a directory out on the internet for them, every time you open it. Personal is meant to be the edition that asks for as little as possible, so those screens are gone rather than quietened — and no requests for them are made. If that is a trade you would rather not make, the full edition has them →

Podcast search still works: type a show's name and Personal asks the podcast directories for it, which it tells you before it does. The full detail of what leaves your phone, and when, is written out in the privacy policy.

The same in both editions

Everything else is simply the app

There is one codebase and one set of screens. Every feature below behaves identically in Mediagg and Mediagg Personal — a fix to the player, the queue or the library reaches both at once.

🎵

Local media, properly organised

Everything on your device, sorted the way a music app should sort it — songs, albums, videos, and a real file browser with breadcrumbs and storage volumes.

🗄️

Your own media servers

Sign in to Subsonic, Navidrome, Airsonic, OpenSubsonic, Jellyfin or Audiobookshelf — several at once — and browse albums, films and shows with artwork, search and offline copies.

🎙️

A serious podcast player

Subscriptions, queue, inbox and downloads, with chapters and follow-along transcripts, automatic downloads, OPML import and export, and gpodder.net sync.

📚

Playlists that are yours

Create, rename, reorder and delete them in the app, or import the M3U playlists you already have. Add anything from any screen.

🎚️

Playback that behaves

Variable speed, skip silence, a sleep timer, full queue control with swipe actions, and lock-screen and notification controls that work.

🎤

Lyrics, in time

From an .lrc file beside the track, from the file's own tags, or from your server — timed line by line wherever the source says when a line is sung.

📡

Casting without brand loyalty

Chromecast, and every DLNA/UPnP renderer on the network. A built-in media relay means files on your phone reach the device as readily as tracks on your server.

🔍

One search, everything at once

Your device, your subscriptions and every signed-in server queried together, in one list — with a chip to scope it down when you know where to look.

⬇️

Downloads that stay put

Keep what you want on the device and play it with no signal at all. Every screen fills from its saved copy first, so losing the network never blanks it.

🎨

Make it yours

Reorder or hide every home-screen section, choose a bottom bar or a side drawer, grid or list and how many columns, in light, dark or true-black.

🚗

Android Auto, Wear OS, widgets

Browse and control from the dashboard, your wrist or the home screen, with the same library underneath and no second setup.

📊

Statistics and backup

See what you actually played, by year, computed on the device. Export the whole database, or your subscriptions as OPML, to a file you choose.
