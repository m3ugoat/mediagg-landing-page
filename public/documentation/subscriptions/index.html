Subscriptions

Podcasts, station playlists and everything else you follow, and the ways to add them.

A subscription is anything Mediagg goes back to and checks again: a podcast, a playlist of stations, or a catalogue you have signed in to. Every one of them ends up in the same library and plays through the same player.

Everything here starts from one screen — Add subscription — which you reach from the Library, or from Get started the first time you open the app.
