Downloads and storage

Keeping episodes on the device, fetching them automatically, and making room again.

Anything streamed can also be kept on the device, which is the difference between a train journey with something to listen to and one without.

Downloading is deliberate by default — you choose what to keep. These pages also cover the automatic side, and what happens when the space you allowed runs out.
