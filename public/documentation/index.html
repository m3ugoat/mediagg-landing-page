How to use Mediagg — getting started, your library, media servers, playback and settings.

Mediagg plays music, podcasts and video from three places at once: files on your device, your own media server, and anything on the open web that has an address. One library, one player, one queue.

These pages cover what each part of the app is for. If you are opening Mediagg for the first time, start with Getting started and come back here when you want the detail.
