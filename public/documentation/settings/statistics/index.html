Statistics

AndroidiOS

How much you have listened to, to what, and over what period.

Statistics, under Your listening in Settings, is How much you have listened to, and to what.

Three views

ChipWhat it counts
ListeningTime played, per subscription
DownloadsSpace used, per subscription
MonthsListening charted month by month

Listening and Downloads list your subscriptions ranked, with a line under each: time played and how many episodes of that show you have started, or space used and how many episodes it covers.

Over what period

Four windows: All time, This year, Last 30 days and Last 7 days.

This year means the year where you are, not where a server is — which is the one thing about a date window people notice being wrong, and only in January.

Counting what you never played

Count episodes marked as played is a switch, and its own line says what it does: Uses their length as the time, even if you never played them.

Off, the figures are time you actually listened. On, an episode you swiped away as done counts its full length. Neither is wrong — the first answers "how long did I listen", the second "how much did I get through".

Empty

Nothing played in this period yet. means exactly that — narrow the window or widen it. The Months chart says Nothing has been played yet, so there is nothing to chart. until there is something to draw.
