Documentation/Settings

Settings

Appearance, the tabs along the bottom, synchronisation, backup and what gets scanned.

Settings is grouped by what a thing is about rather than by when it was added: what applies to everything comes first, then one part for each kind of library you have.

A setting that cannot mean anything on your device is not shown at all, so this screen is shorter on some phones than others.

AppearanceLight, dark and black, and the three places the colours can come from.What the bar showsChoosing which tabs appear along the bottom, ordering them, and what happens past five.SynchronisationKeeping podcast subscriptions and positions in step with your other devices.BackupTaking a copy of your library and your server list, and bringing one over.StatisticsHow much you have listened to, to what, and over what period.ScanningWhich kinds of audio and which folders on the device end up in your library.
