**OPML is the file every podcast app uses to hand over a list of subscriptions.** Export one from
the app you are leaving, then open `Import subscriptions from a file` on the `Add subscription`
screen and pick it.

## Choosing what to bring

Mediagg reads the file and shows you what is in it before adding anything. Nothing is subscribed to
until you choose — the button counts your selection, so it reads `Choose what to add` until you have
picked something and then `Add 12`.

That step matters more than it sounds. A list exported after several years of listening usually
contains shows you stopped caring about, and this is the easiest moment to leave them behind.

## Not everything will arrive

When it has finished, Mediagg says how many of them worked — added so many of so many — and then why:

> **`The rest did not answer. An export is a snapshot, and some of what it lists has moved or gone.`**

That is normal, and it is not a fault in the file. Podcasts stop publishing and feeds change
address, and an OPML file records where a show was on the day it was exported.

## If the file will not open

`There are no subscriptions in that file, or it could not be read.` means Mediagg found nothing it
recognised. Check that you exported subscriptions rather than, say, a playlist, and that the file
arrived whole.
