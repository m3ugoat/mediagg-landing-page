Open `Add subscription` from the Library, and two of its rows are about finding a podcast you cannot
name an address for.

## Browsing what is popular

`Browse top podcasts` shows what is popular, by country and category. It is the best starting point
if you are looking for something to listen to rather than something in particular.

Tapping a show opens it before you commit — you can read the description and see the recent episodes,
and subscribe from there or back out.

## Searching by name

Searching asks several podcast directories at once and merges the answers, so a show that only one of
them lists still comes back. Where a directory is worth asking on its own, the screen also offers
`Search` for that directory by name — useful when one of them has better coverage of what you are
after.

Results are merged by how highly each directory ranked them, not by which directory answered first.

> **Subscribing is not the only way to listen.** Opening a show from a search result plays its
> episodes without adding it to your library. Subscribe when you want new episodes to keep arriving.

## What happens when you subscribe

The show is added to your library under `Subscriptions`, and Mediagg starts checking it for new
episodes on the schedule you have set — see [Refreshing](/documentation/subscriptions/refreshing).

New episodes appear on the `New` chip and in the **What is new** shelf on Home. Whether they are
also downloaded automatically is a separate choice; see
[Automatic downloads](/documentation/downloads/automatic-downloads).
