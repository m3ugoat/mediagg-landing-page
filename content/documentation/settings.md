Settings is grouped by what a thing is about rather than by when it was added: **what applies to
everything comes first** — under `Common` — then `Home`, then one part for each kind of library you
have: `Subscriptions`, `Local media`, `Media servers` and `Downloads`.

`Common` ends with `About` and `Diagnostics`, which hold the version, the privacy notice, the crash
logs and `Report an issue` — see
[Reporting a problem](/documentation/help/report-a-problem). Where the app is sold through a store,
a row about supporting it sits above everything — see
[Supporting Mediagg](/documentation/settings/supporting).

A setting that cannot mean anything on your device is not shown at all, so this screen is shorter on
some phones than others.
