The bar along the bottom starts with three: `Home`, `Library` and `Search`. Plus `Settings`, which
is always there and cannot be removed.

**Mediagg carries more kinds of content than a bar has room for**, so which tabs you get is yours to
choose. Open Settings and find `What the bar shows`.

## What you can promote

Seven of the Library's sections can be lifted out onto the bar:

- `Subscriptions`
- `Local media`
- `Media servers`
- `Playlists`
- `Favourites`
- `Downloads`
- `History`

These are shortcuts, not new screens. A promoted tab opens the same section the Library already
lists, which saves two taps rather than creating a second place for the same content to live.

## Past five

**The bar holds five.** Choose more than that and the extras move into a `More` sheet at the end —
and because `More` takes a slot of its own, the overflow starts one early.

> **An arrangement naming something that is no longer there is not rejected.** If a tab you had
> chosen has since been folded into another screen, it is quietly dropped from your arrangement and
> the rest is kept.

## Search

Search looks across everything at once: your subscriptions, what is on the device, and the libraries
of any servers you have signed in to. It is a tab by default because it is the fastest route to
something you know you have.
