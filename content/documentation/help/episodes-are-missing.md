title: Episodes have disappeared
summary: Where downloaded episodes go when the space you allowed runs out, and how to keep them.

Episodes vanishing is almost always **Mediagg doing what you told it to**. Two settings delete
things, and both are under `Downloads`.

## The limit was reached

`Keep at most` puts a ceiling on how many downloaded episodes are held. When a new one arrives and
the ceiling is already reached, something has to go — and `When the limit is reached, delete` says
what.

Set `Keep at most` to `As many as fit` to stop this happening by count.

## Which one went

The four policies under `Making room`, and what each spares:

| Policy | What it deletes |
|---|---|
| `Nothing` | Nothing is ever deleted automatically |
| `Anything not in the queue` | Queued episodes are safe |
| `Anything but favourites` | Favourited episodes are safe |
| `Episodes you have finished` | Only what you have played through |

**Favouriting an episode is the simplest way to keep it.** Under `Anything but favourites`, a
favourite is never taken.

## It went right after I finished it

`Wait after playing` decides how long a finished episode is kept before it becomes eligible.
`Straight away` means exactly that. Give it a few hours if you tend to re-listen.

## The episode is still in the list, just not downloaded

Deleting a download does not unsubscribe you and does not remove the episode. It goes back to being
something you stream or download again — the row stays.

## Local files

`Include folders on this device` decides whether the same clearing-out applies to media you added
from a folder rather than downloaded. **Leave it off if those files are the only copy**, because
they are not something Mediagg can fetch again.

> **`Nothing` is a real choice.** If you would rather manage space yourself, set the policy to
> `Nothing` and delete downloads by hand from the `Downloads` section.
