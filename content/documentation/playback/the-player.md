Start anything and a **mini player** appears above the bar along the bottom: the artwork, what is
playing, and enough controls to pause and skip without leaving the screen you are on.

**Everything plays through this one player** — a podcast episode, a track off your phone, a live
station, something on a server. There is no separate screen per kind of media.

## Opening it out

The mini player opens into the full player, and it is **dragged rather than switched**. Pull it up
and it follows your finger: the artwork grows, the controls arrive, and the bar along the bottom
slides down out of the way as the player rises to take its place.

Because it is a drag, it can be part-way. Let go before the top and it settles back.

## What is on the full player

The artwork, the title and who it is by, a position bar you can scrub, and the transport controls.
What else appears depends on what is playing — an episode with chapters offers them, one with a
transcript offers that, a track with lyrics offers those.

Live stations are the exception to scrubbing: **there is no position to move to**, so the bar shows
what is being broadcast now rather than a place in a recording. Many stations announce the current
track, and where they do, Mediagg shows the record rather than the station's own name.

## The queue underneath

The queue is its own sheet inside the player, with its own peek and its own drag. Pull it up from
the bottom of the full player to see what is coming and reorder it. See
[The queue](/documentation/playback/the-queue).

> **Closing the player does not stop playback.** Dragging it back down returns you to whatever screen
> you were on, with the mini player still there. Playback stops when you stop it.
