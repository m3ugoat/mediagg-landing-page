`Keep at most` sets a ceiling on downloads. `When the limit is reached, delete`, under
`Making room`, decides what happens when a new episode arrives and the ceiling is already reached.

## The four policies

| Policy | What it deletes |
|---|---|
| `Nothing` | `Downloads are only ever removed by you` |
| `Anything not in the queue` | `Oldest first. Favourites are always kept` |
| `Anything but favourites` | `Oldest first, the queue included` |
| `Episodes you have finished` | `Least recently finished first, once they have sat a while` |

**Favourites are safe under three of the four.** Favouriting an episode is the simplest way to keep
something you are not ready to lose.

`Nothing` is a real choice, not a way of switching the feature off badly: it means you manage the
space yourself, and nothing is ever taken without you asking.

## Waiting after you have finished

With `Episodes you have finished`, a second row appears: `Wait after playing`. It offers
`Straight away`, `An hour`, `12 hours`, `A day`, `3 days` and `A week`.

**`Straight away` means exactly that** — finish an episode and it becomes eligible immediately. Give
it a day or two if you re-listen, or if you like to check something you heard earlier in the week.

## Your own files

`Include folders on this device` appears under every policy except `Nothing`, and its line is the
argument for leaving it off:

> **`Those files are yours, not a copy of something a server still has`**

A download can always be fetched again. A file in a folder you added cannot — if Mediagg deletes it,
it is gone. Turn this on only if you are sure those files exist somewhere else too.

## Nothing is being deleted

Check that `Keep at most` is not set to `As many as fit`, which means there is no limit to reach and
therefore nothing to clear. And a policy of `Nothing` will never delete, whatever the ceiling says.
