A subscription is anything Mediagg goes back to and checks again: a podcast, a playlist of stations,
or a catalogue you have signed in to. **Every one of them ends up in the same library** and plays
through the same player.

Everything here starts from one screen — `Add subscription` — which you reach from the Library, or
from `Get started` the first time you open the app.
