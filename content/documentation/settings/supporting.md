Mediagg has no adverts and no trackers. The support screen says it plainly:
`Mediagg has no adverts and no trackers, which means the only thing paying for it is people choosing
to. Any of these helps, and none of them is expected.`

**A tip is a thank-you, not a key.** Nothing in the app is held back from anybody who has not given.

## Where it is

At the very top of Settings, above `Common`: `Enjoying Mediagg?` —
`Tap to see various ways you can support us to keep delivering better features.` It opens
`Support Mediagg`.

The row is there only where the app is sold through a store — the Google Play build and the App
Store build. A build that cannot take a payment does not show a row leading to a screen with no
prices on it.

## The two kinds

| Heading | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Support every month` | `Bronze`, `Silver` or `Gold`, billed monthly until you cancel |
| `Give once` | The same three tiers as a single tip — `A one-off thank you. Give again whenever you like.` |

Every price is the store's, in your own currency, and a tier the store does not quote is simply not
listed.

The three monthly tiers give exactly the same thing, for different amounts:
`Alternate app icons and a supporter badge while it runs. Cancel whenever you like.`

## What you get back

- **A badge.** Settings' top row becomes `Thank you —` followed by your badge, with a medal in your
  tier's colour: `Gold supporter` for a monthly one, or `Gave a Gold tip` for a one-off. `About Mediagg`
  shows the same under the version.
- **App icons, for a monthly supporter.** An `App icon` row appears under `Appearance` — see
  [Appearance](/documentation/settings/appearance). It lasts as long as the monthly support does.

## Already paid?

`Restore purchases` —
`Ask the store what this account owns — after a reinstall, or on a new device.` Purchases belong to
your store account, not to the phone, so a new device only needs asking.

> **A payment the store is still waiting on is not lost.** `The store is waiting for your payment.
> This will update as soon as it goes through.` If something goes wrong instead, the screen says
> `The store could not complete that. Nothing has been charged.`
