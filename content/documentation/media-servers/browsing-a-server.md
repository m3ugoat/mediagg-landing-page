Open `Media servers` and the server's own content is read live. Two chips split it:

- **`Activity`** — shelves of what is recent, popular and part-finished. This is where it opens.
- **`Library`** — a grid of the places on the server to browse into.

## Shelves

Shelves are named consistently whatever your server calls them — `Recently added`, `Most played`,
`Continue watching`, `Latest films`, `Books`, `Playlists` and so on. **Only shelves with something
in them are drawn.**

Each heading has a *See all*, and where its contents are playable, `Play all` and `Shuffle all`
beside it.

Shelves load as you scroll to them rather than all at once, which is what keeps a large library from
being a long wait. Pull down to refresh past the saved copy.

## Inside a folder or a shelf

Tracks draw as a list, films and albums as a grid of covers. A sort control offers `As listed`,
`Name`, and — where the items carry them — `Rating` and `Year`.

`As listed` is the server's own order, which for a playlist is usually the one you want.

## Detail pages

**An album, a playlist or an audiobook** opens to its cover, a facts line giving its year,
how many tracks and how long it runs, `Play` and `Shuffle`, and its contents. The word follows the thing:
chapters for a book, episodes for a season.

**An artist** opens to a portrait, their albums, top tracks, who they appear alongside, similar
artists, an `Artist mix` button and a link to look them up on Wikipedia.

**A film or an episode** opens to its poster, tagline, overview, genres, director, writers, studios
and cast.

## A film opens, a track plays

Tapping a track plays it. **Tapping a film opens it** — because a tap on a poster is asking for the
overview, the cast and the running time, not for two hours to start.

Where the server holds no record for a film, there is nothing to open, so it plays instead.

## Where you left off

`Resume` appears in place of `Play` when pressing it would genuinely carry on.

**That position is Mediagg's own record of where you got to**, not the server's guess. It means a
book you were part-way through before you added the account still resumes correctly, and a server
with progress reporting switched off does not lose your place.

Two accounts on the same server are kept apart: a book one of them has begun is not one the other
has.

> **A part-played album still says `Play`.** Albums of songs start from the top — resuming applies
> to books and to video, where carrying on is what you meant.
