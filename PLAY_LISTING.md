## Full description

```
Mediagg plays music, podcasts, radio, audiobooks and video from three places at once: the files on your phone, your own media server, and anything on the open web that has an address. One library, one player, one queue.

YOUR OWN MEDIA SERVER
Mediagg is a client for Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, Audiobookshelf and Subsonic servers (Navidrome, Airsonic, Gonic and others). Your library stays where it is: nothing is copied to the phone unless you download it, and your progress is reported back so the server and your other players stay in step.
• Browse recently added, most played and continue watching shelves, or the server's full library
• Sign in to several servers and read them as one combined library
• Mixes built from your server's own suggestions, one tap to start
• Read EPUB, comic and PDF books from Audiobookshelf, with your place saved to the server

PODCASTS
• Search several podcast directories at once, or browse top podcasts by country and category
• Add any feed by address, share a link to Mediagg from any app, or import an OPML file from the app you are leaving
• Chapters, show notes and transcripts, shown only when an episode has them
• Automatic downloads of new episodes that make room for themselves when space runs low
• Refresh on a schedule, on Wi-Fi only if you prefer
• Keep subscriptions and positions in sync with gpodder.net or Nextcloud

LIVE RADIO AND TV
• Subscribe to station playlists (M3U) and every station in them becomes something you can play
• Read playlists from any public GitHub directory, kept up to date as it changes
• Paste a stream address and Mediagg looks at what is actually there, so radio, feeds and playlists all go in the same field
• Works with a self-hosted m3ugoat server for your own arranged IPTV playlists

YOUR FILES
• Every song and video on your device, by artist, album, genre, year or folder
• Timed lyrics from your files or from LrcLib, following the music line by line
• Playlists from M3U files or addresses

ONE PLAYER FOR EVERYTHING
• A podcast episode, a track from your phone, a live station and a film on your server can all sit in the same queue
• Mini player that drags up into the full player, with the queue one more pull away
• Playback speed and a sleep timer
• Skip intros, recaps and credits on server video
• Video in the player or in picture-in-picture
• Cast to Chromecast, Google TV and DLNA televisions and receivers

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light, dark, or true black for OLED screens
• Accent colours from your wallpaper, from what is playing, or one colour of your choice
• Listening statistics: time played per show, space used per download, and month by month
• Back up your library, play state and queue to a file, and restore it on another device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Mediagg talks to your servers and to the feeds and services you choose, and nothing else. Crash logs stay on your phone; Report an issue opens a prefilled GitHub page that you choose whether to submit.

SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT
Mediagg is made by one developer. If it earns a place on your phone, you can leave a one-off tip or become a monthly supporter, which unlocks alternative app icons and a supporter badge.

Mediagg is not affiliated with Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, Audiobookshelf, Navidrome or any other server it connects to. You need your own server, or access to one, to use the media server features.
```
